## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Carousel supports touch and drag
Visitors SHALL be able to move through the project cards by swiping on a touch screen or dragging with a pointer, in addition to the Previous and Next buttons.

#### Scenario: Swiping on a phone
- **WHEN** a visitor on a phone swipes left on the project cards
- **THEN** the carousel advances to the next card

#### Scenario: Buttons still work
- **WHEN** a visitor presses Previous or Next
- **THEN** the carousel moves one step in that direction
